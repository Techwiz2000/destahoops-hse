About DestaHoops Hse

More Than a Training Facility — A Basketball Family

Founded by former pro players, DestaHoops Hse combines modern skill development with mentorship. We believe every athlete deserves personalized attention, real feedback, and a culture that demands effort and rewards growth.

🎯

Personalized Development

Every player gets an individualized skill assessment and a progressive training plan reviewed every 6 weeks.

🏀

Game-Real Reps

Small-sided games, situational drills and live scrimmages so skills transfer directly to game night.

🧠

Character First

Discipline, teamwork, leadership and academic accountability are coached as seriously as footwork.

Our Programs

Find Your Lane

From beginners to collegiate hopefuls, there's a track built for your goals.

Ages 6–10

Little Ballers Academy

Fun-first fundamentals: coordination, dribbling, shooting form and a love for the game. 2 sessions/week.

Ages 11–14

Skills Development Camp

Position-specific skills, finishing, defense and decision-making. Includes weekly scrimmage league.

Ages 15–18

Elite Prep Squad

High-intensity training for school and club players. Strength & conditioning, film study and exposure prep.

1-on-1

Private Coaching

Hour-long private sessions tailored to your exact weaknesses, with video breakdown and progress reports.

Teams

Team Training Packages

Bring your whole squad. Systems installation, chemistry drills and coach-the-coaches support.

Adults

Adult Hoops Fitness

Cardio basketball for grown-ups who miss the game. Competitive runs every Friday night.
